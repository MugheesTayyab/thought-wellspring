# BajiHears - Project Vision & Future Development Ideas

This document captures the ultimate vision and roadmap for BajiHears, directly from the founder. Future development should revolve around these ideas.

## The First Impression

When a user arrives from the comment section and opens the link, the very first thing they should see is a pinned, highly relatable story (the "Last 24 Hour Winner") shared by someone (handle included).

## Writing Section & Options

- The writing section should be placed immediately underneath the winner card, ensuring users cannot easily leave without writing their own thoughts.
- Instead of generic "your text here" placeholder text, use something engaging like "your words matter" to attract interaction.
- The writing section should have a color picker circle on the left, allowing users to switch the text box color instantly.
- When a user clicks to write, additional options seamlessly slide in underneath:
  1. **Identity Toggle**: Two minimal options side-by-side for posting as "Anonymous" or "@handle".
  2. **Niche / Category Selection**: A third row showing text categories (e.g., Personal Story, Made-up Thought, Random Thought, Personal Experience, Best Quote, etc.).

## The Winner Experience (Main Box)

- The main box highlights the "Last 24 Hour Winner".
- It should display the time remaining to post on the account (e.g., "Will be posting on Instagram in [Time]").
- The winner should have a striking, catchy label (like "new experience" or "left everyone speechless") to highlight its quality.
- Users can comment on the winning story (limited to 1 comment per story).
- Liking should support Instagram-style double-click or tapping a like button.

## Scrolling & Interactions

- **3D Background**: The background should be static but feel very 3D or slightly movable on scroll.
- **Infinite Feed**: Non-stop scroll with different colored animations for cards.
- **The Baji Mascot**:
  - There should be a "Baji" emoji or hand that pushes the feed up and down, perfectly synced to the user's scroll motion. (e.g., If you scroll up, Baji instantly grabs and pushes the screen forward). This creates a highly interactive feel.
  - Initial Load: A very fast 3-second smooth "BajiHears" animation plays when the website opens, before transitioning to the feed.
  - The Baji character on the side should be clickable. When clicked, it expands into an LLM (knowing everything about Baji) that answers questions after a 4-second typing animation ("Baji is typing...").
  - The AI Baji interaction should have a context window but be limited to 3 questions a day.

## Profile & Personalization

- On the top right, instead of a plain avatar, there should be a circle containing a crazy, unique doll profile.
- Clicking it opens options for:
  1. Sharing.
  2. Profile adjustment (framed in unique wording, like "New Experience").

## Sharing & Curation

- Users should be able to copy the text or share it as an aesthetic Instagram Story.
- The platform should provide high-quality, aesthetic presets with unique and catchy names that users can choose from when sharing.

## Phase 2: Instagram Integration

- Start a series sharing 5 personal stories daily on Instagram.
- The best story of the day will be placed on Instagram Highlights with mentions.
- Keep the platform frictionless for now: no authentication required, just start with anonymous posting.

## Phase 3: Interactive "Which one is you?" Features

- Introduce A/B interactive posts with two text boxes side-by-side (e.g., "Middle class things like kicking an ice cream cover before throwing").
- Ask users: "Are you or are you not?" or "Which one hits harder?" for direct engagement.
