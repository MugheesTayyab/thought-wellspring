================================================================================
BAJIHEARS DESIGN FIXES & ANTI-AI-SLOP QUALITY LOG
================================================================================
Purpose: This log records every anti-pattern and AI-generated design tell
identified and eliminated from BajiHears. Every new feature, UI tweak, and copy
change must be vetted against this checklist before shipping to production.

Target Audience & Context:
Direct link-in from Instagram bio with ~10,000 active users. All interfaces must
exude authentic, candlelit, premium intimacy—no robotic tropes or stock kits.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. TYPOGRAPHY & FONT CONSISTENCY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[IDENTIFIED ANTI-PATTERN]
- Spraying `font-mono` across non-code UI elements (e.g. "+2 warmth earned",
  unlock progress "0/5", match confidence "96% Match", trait breakdowns, labels).
  Monospace in mainstream consumer/teen social apps feels clinical, cold, and
  screams "lazy AI boilerplate".
- Inconsistent decorative fonts for winner labels ("Cycle Champion") that looked
  like stock templates rather than the brand's typographic identity.

[PRODUCTION RULE & FIX]
- Primary Copy: Warm, clean system sans (`font-sans`).
- Brand & Display Headers: Warm serif display (`font-display`, Cormorant/serif).
- Confession & Literary Quotes: Atmospheric quote font (`font-quote`).
- Numerical Metrics & Counts: Always use `font-sans font-bold tabular-nums text-white`
  or warm neutral tones. Numbers align cleanly without monospace robotic aesthetics.
- Audited and purged `font-mono` from:
  * WarmthToast & earn notifications
  * WarmthSheet & Points Balance
  * LockedTabModal progress counters
  * BajiReadLockedCard status counters
  * BajiReadCard trait breakdown percentages & badges
  * PublicProfileModal header pills
  * WarmthStore balance badges and limited-item pill tags

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. PUNCTUATION & AI SYNTAX (EM DASH PURGE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[IDENTIFIED ANTI-PATTERN]
- Excessive use of em dashes ("—") in user-facing copy, subtitles, and button
  labels. In modern web and mobile copy, heavy em-dash cadence is one of the most
  notorious telltale markers of unedited LLM-generated prose.

[PRODUCTION RULE & FIX]
- Absolute zero em dashes in user-facing UI, toast notifications, badges, or copy.
- Use natural sentence structures, clean periods, or standard hyphens ("-") only
  when strictly required for hyphenated adjectives (e.g., "candle-lit").
- Rephrased all teaser, unlock, and psychology paragraphs into concise, human,
  spoken cadence.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. FLASHING, PULSING & SHIMMER GIMMICKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[IDENTIFIED ANTI-PATTERN]
- Repeating flashing/shimmering animations (`animate-pulse`) on either side of
  "Instant Unlock" or "Open Duel" badges and lock icons.
- Constant pulsing creates visual agitation, looks like a cheap mobile game ad,
  and distracts from the core content.
- Unnecessary AI sparkles (`✨`, `⭐`) attached as filler decoration.

[PRODUCTION RULE & FIX]
- Replace all repeating strobe/pulse effects with restrained static highlights or
  a single, minimal hover transition (`border-white/10 bg-white/[0.04] hover:bg-white/[0.08]`).
- Instant Unlock is styled as a dignified glass button with a subtle static
  amber icon (`Zap className="text-amber-400"`), no pulsating halos.
- Lock icons use a static, soft candlelight ember backdrop without `animate-pulse`.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. COPYWRITING, GAMIFICATION & COGNITIVE LOAD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[IDENTIFIED ANTI-PATTERN]
- Vague, pseudo-mystical jargon ("aura", "warmth", "ecological read"). First-time
  users could not immediately understand what warmth was, why they had it, or what
  they could do with it.
- "+2 warmth earned" displayed in an abrasive green terminal font that clashed
  with the dark warm candlelight palette.
- Overlong, breathless run-on explanations that overwhelmed incoming visitors in
  their first 5 seconds.

[PRODUCTION RULE & FIX]
- Visibility of System Status & Plain Language:
  * Renamed section headers to "Points & Status" and "Perks & Store".
  * Added one concise, immediately parseable definition:
    "Points you earn by sharing thoughts and voting, used to unlock personality reads and special perks."
  * Renamed "Total Warmth" to "Points Balance" with crisp white tabular numbers.
  * Replaced "+2 warmth earned" green text with a glowing candlelight pill
    ("+2 points earned" in `font-sans font-bold tabular-nums text-white`).
  * Renamed "PSYCHOLOGICAL READ" / "ecological read" to "2 MIN READ · PERSONALITY INSIGHT"
    (plain language conveying estimated read time and content type).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. UNLOCK PROGRESS & CALLS TO ACTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[IDENTIFIED ANTI-PATTERN]
- Bare, context-free fractions like "0/3" or "0/5" without telling the user
  what action was required to advance the counter.

[PRODUCTION RULE & FIX]
- Every unlock bar must state the exact, actionable imperative:
  * Duel: "Vote in 3 duels to unlock" accompanied by "X of 3 completed"
  * Read: "Read 5 posts to unlock" accompanied by "X of 5 completed"
- Counter rendered in clean, high-contrast white font (`text-white font-bold tabular-nums`).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. MATCH & INSIGHT BADGES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[IDENTIFIED ANTI-PATTERN]
- Ad-hoc, unpolished badges like a green-dot pulsating ".94% match" or a single
  dense string of traits joined by bullets ("Observant Radar • High Intuition").

[PRODUCTION RULE & FIX]
- Match Confidence: Restrained warm amber pill (`border-amber-500/25 bg-amber-500/10 text-amber-300`)
  clearly labeled: "{matchAccuracy}% Personality Match".
- Insight Tags: Distinct, individual glassmorphic badges with candlelight dot
  accents (`Observant`, `High Intuition`, `Silent Boundaries`), matching industry
  benchmarks from Spotify Wrapped and Hinge.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. APP LOAD SEQUENCE & FOUC ELIMINATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[IDENTIFIED ANTI-PATTERN]
- Flash of unstyled or partially loaded feed before the intro animation mounted.
  Caused by initializing splash visibility to `false` and waiting for an
  asynchronous client `useEffect` to inspect `sessionStorage`.

[PRODUCTION RULE & FIX]
- Synchronous initialization: `BajiIntroSplash` initializes `visible` to `true`
  immediately if `sessionStorage.getItem("bh:introPlayed")` is absent.
- Parent mount gating: The main feed in `src/routes/index.tsx` is held at
  `opacity-0 pointer-events-none` while the intro plays behind a high-z-index
  dark canvas, fading smoothly to `opacity-100` only upon `onComplete`.
- Redesigned Intro Animation:
  * Replaced generic loader with organic candlelight flame glow.
  * Floating, borderless Baji mascot emblem.
  * Timed 4-word poetic value proposition: "Whisper your truth. Read the unsaid."
  * Hairline smooth progress bar.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. GLOBAL GLASSMORPHISM STANDARDIZATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[IDENTIFIED ANTI-PATTERN]
- Modals and sheets using inconsistent backdrops (`bg-black/75`, `backdrop-blur-md`)
  and flat dark boxes (`bg-[#17110D]/95`) while other popups had rich gradients.

[PRODUCTION RULE & FIX]
- Uniform Overlay Design System:
  * Backdrop: `bg-black/85 backdrop-blur-xl animate-[fadeIn_0.15s_ease-out]`
  * Container: `border border-white/12 bg-gradient-to-b from-[#1f1712]/95 via-[#17110D]/95 to-[#100b08]/98 shadow-[0_20px_60px_-15px_rgba(0,0,0,0.9)] backdrop-blur-2xl rounded-3xl`
  * Standardized across `LockedTabModal`, `BajiReadLockedCard`, `PublicProfileModal`,
    `QuoteCardDialog`, `PushPermissionSheet`, `GiftMilestone`, `BajiReadShareDialog`,
    and `WarmthStore`.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. GOOGLE ACCOUNT CONNECTION & OAUTH BRANDING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[IDENTIFIED ANTI-PATTERN]
- Google sign-in consent screen showing internal/placeholder project names
  ("continue to QSLOQQ event" referencing internal Supabase project ids).

[PRODUCTION RULE & FIX]
- Code-level: Added `queryParams: { prompt: "select_account", access_type: "offline" }`
  in `auth-context.tsx` to force the clean Google account selection sheet.
- Configuration-level: In Google Cloud Console (APIs & Services > OAuth consent screen),
  update the public App Name to "BajiHears" and set user support email to the
  production administrator contact so no internal project IDs appear.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. GESTURE CONFLICT RESOLUTION (DOUBLE-TAP VS. COPY)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[IDENTIFIED ANTI-PATTERN]
- Double-tapping a post accidentally triggered both browser/app copy and the
  like animation simultaneously, causing jarring feedback.

[PRODUCTION RULE & FIX]
- Gesture decoupling: Single card taps and double taps are completely isolated
  from copy actions. Double tap exclusively triggers `onReact(id, "heart")` and
  the minimal like heart pop. Copy is accessible strictly via the 3-dot overflow
  menu or long-press.
- Minimal like animation: Scaled down from `size-14` to a delicate `size-9` heart
  pop with low motion and quick 0.28s settle that never obscures body text.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
11. POST CARD METADATA & INSTAGRAM POSITIONING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[IDENTIFIED ANTI-PATTERN]
- Date stamp sitting awkwardly over body text or conflicting with badges.
- Instagram logo placed inconsistently to the left of the handle in some views.

[PRODUCTION RULE & FIX]
- Standard card header conventions:
  * Top metadata row hosts author handle, Instagram icon to the right of username,
    clean middot separator, and relative time (`2h ago`), with category tag on right.
  * Post quote body is rendered cleanly below the header, ensuring the date never
    overlaps or sits on top of body text.
  * Echo lists consistently position the Instagram icon to the right of the handle.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
12. FEED SEQUENCE & INTERSPERSED COMMUNITY LEADERBOARD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[IDENTIFIED ANTI-PATTERN]
- Rigidly pinning Community Regulars directly below the writing box and sorting
  toggles, cluttering the top of the feed for returning visitors.

[PRODUCTION RULE & FIX]
- Removed Community Regulars from fixed header sequence.
- Interspersed naturally every 12 posts (`index % 12 === 0`) as the user scrolls,
  providing fresh social proof without obstructing the initial writing flow.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
13. SCROLL-SYNCED BADGE ACCURACY & SNAP DEBOUNCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[IDENTIFIED ANTI-PATTERN]
- Side mascot or badges lagging behind scroll instead of snapping the instant
  scrolling stops, caused by large arbitrary timeouts.

[PRODUCTION RULE & FIX]
- Native `scrollend` event support with a tight 50ms fallback debounce.
- Instant velocity decay on stop so the resting frame snaps with zero perceptible lag.
- Winner badge given a subtle 3D physical float (`perspective(600px) translateZ(12px)`)
  rather than flat generic bounce.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
14. TEXT WRAPPING & ORPHAN PREVENTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[IDENTIFIED ANTI-PATTERN]
- Short words orphaned onto awkward isolated second lines across quotes and badges.

[PRODUCTION RULE & FIX]
- Global application of `[text-wrap:balance]` and `[text-wrap:pretty]` across all
  post quotes, teaser cards, headers, and badges.
- Native CSS layout balances lines naturally across all device viewports.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
15. SELECTION CRITERIA & INTROVERT ONBOARDING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[IDENTIFIED ANTI-PATTERN]
- Unclear path to getting featured on Instagram / Bajislays; lack of explicit
  selection criteria for both editorial curators and audience voting.

[PRODUCTION RULE & FIX]
- High-visibility onboarding in both `HeroIntro` and Wall Empty State:
  * Dedicated to introverts with a loud mind: anonymous thoughts reach thousands.
  * Explicit criteria: Curators judge raw authenticity, writing craft, and emotional weight.
  * Audience votes on relatability, emotional resonance, and shared human truth.
