You're reading the Wall and someone has written something that stops you. You want to say
something, and everything you think of sounds either hollow or like too much. This is what I've
come to believe about replying to a stranger.

[YOU: A reply someone once gave you that really helped, or one that really hurt. Or the best Echo

## Respond to what they actually wrote

The kindest replies show that you read it. "The part about still setting a plate for him stayed
with me" says far more than "stay strong". You don't need wisdom, only proof that a person read it.

## Things that usually land badly

- **"Everything happens for a reason."** You don't know that, and neither do they.
- **"At least..."** Anything after "at least" tends to shrink what they just told you.
- **"You should..."** Advice nobody asked for can sound like you've stopped listening.
- **"I went through worse."** Sharing your experience can help. Competing with theirs won't.
- **Guessing who they are, or who the other person is.** Even a good guess can do real damage.

## Things that usually help

- "I read this, and I'm glad you wrote it."
- "That sounds so heavy."
- "I've felt something close to this, when..."
- "You don't owe anyone an explanation for this."

Short is fine. An Echo can only be 200 characters, and that's usually enough.

## If you're worried about them

You can't diagnose someone from one post, and you aren't responsible for saving a stranger. If a
post makes you think someone might be in danger, do two things:

1. Use **Report** in the post's menu so it reaches moderation.
2. If you reply, say you care, and point them to the [help page](/help). Don't promise anything you
   can't follow through on.

Posts are anonymous, so usually nobody can track the person down and check on them. That's hard to
accept, but it's better to know it now than to assume someone else is handling it.

## You can also just read

Not replying is fine. Pressing Relate is fine too. Nobody here owes anyone a response, including
you, and that's part of what makes it possible for people to write honestly.
