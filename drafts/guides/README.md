# Guide drafts: not ready to publish

These are starting points, not finished articles. Nothing in this folder goes on the site.

To publish one:

1. Replace every `[YOU: ...]` with your own story, in your own words. Those parts are what make
   an article original, so don't skip them or ask an AI to invent them.
2. Rewrite anything that doesn't sound like you, and cut anything you don't believe.
3. Check it reaches at least 800 words. The drafts are shorter on purpose, because your part is
   the part that's missing.
4. Open the source links and confirm they say what the article claims.
5. Put your real name in `author`, set today's date, and delete the `draft: true` line.
6. Move the file to `src/content/guides/`, run `npm test`, then deploy.

Publishing these close to as they are would work against you: thin, generic articles are what
Google's low-value and scaled-content rules are aimed at.
